Event 1: Blood Donation Camp

The Blood Donation Camp held last year aimed to contribute to society by collecting blood from willing donors. The camp saw an overwhelming response from students and faculty, all coming together to donate blood for those in need. The event not only helped save lives but also raised awareness about the importance of regular blood donation to maintain a sufficient blood supply in hospitals and medical centers.

Event 2: Eye Check-up

The Eye Check-up event focused on promoting eye health and raising awareness about common eye issues. A team of qualified optometrists conducted comprehensive eye examinations for students and staff. Free eyeglasses were provided to those who needed them. The event emphasized the importance of regular eye check-ups and proper eye care to prevent vision problems and maintain good eye health.

Event 3: Cycle Rally

The Cycle Rally was an eco-friendly initiative to promote cycling as a sustainable mode of transportation and a fun way to stay active. Participants cycled through the city, spreading the message of environmental conservation and reducing carbon emissions. The event garnered support from the local community, inspiring people to opt for cycling for short commutes and promoting a greener lifestyle.

Event 4: Peace Walk

The Peace Walk aimed to spread the message of peace, unity, and harmony among individuals and communities. Participants walked together, carrying banners and placards with messages of love and understanding. The event served as a platform to raise awareness about the importance of resolving conflicts peacefully and promoting a culture of tolerance and inclusivity.
