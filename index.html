    About Us

    The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a permanent youth program under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India, funded by both the Government of Tamil Nadu and the Government of India. The NSS motto is "Not me but you," emphasizing that an individual's well-being is ultimately tied to the welfare of society as a whole. This motto encapsulates the essence of democratic living, emphasizing the importance of selfless service, appreciation for other perspectives, and consideration for fellow human beings. NSS is a voluntary service program that allows students to enhance their personalities through active engagement in social service.
